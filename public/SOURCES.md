# demo2 — modeling notes

The tower, apartments, terrain, planting, and facade details are illustrative. Dimensions and floor layouts are conceptual.

## Neighborhood data

The surrounding footprints and paths use a locally bundled, modified OpenStreetMap snapshot retrieved 15 September 2026. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, distributed under the [Open Database License (ODbL)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The modified snapshot is in `src/data/neighborhood.json`; feature IDs are retained for data provenance. Names and address tags are omitted, and labels are generic. Coordinates are local metres, with X east and Z south. Heights use mapped tags where available and estimates otherwise.

## Floor exploration

The model has 70 concept floors with 12 illustrative apartments per floor. Room layouts, terrace positions, and shared areas are demonstration geometry.
