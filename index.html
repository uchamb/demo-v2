THE PROJECT / THE PLACE

From reference to landscape.

VR Vake Sky Tower · Fashion Avenue

A planned 70-floor, 260 m mixed-use tower at 49 Chavchavadze Avenue, on the former Sports University site. The developer describes residences, offices, retail, restaurants, wellness spaces, underground parking and a rooftop infinity pool. Its project page lists completion in 2031.

How to read this map

Project location
41.7122948° N, 44.7467461° E, from the official website’s map pin. The pin is a project location, not a surveyed footprint.
Neighborhood extent
The scene covers a 1,000 × 940 m area centered on the project, including its nearby streets, buildings and park setting.
Mapped context
Streets, building footprints, sports grounds and green areas come from a local OpenStreetMap snapshot, retrieved 15 September 2026. Heights use mapped height or floor-count tags when present; other heights are estimated.
Architectural interpretation
The tower’s rounded façade, sky gardens and public realm follow the renders. Its footprint, floor spacing, podium, pool and unseen details are illustrative. Terrain, tree positions, road widths, façades and street furniture are approximations, not a survey.
Beyond this view
Vake Park and Mikheil Meskhi Stadium extend beyond the local project site. Turtle Lake, Central Park and the Philharmonic are wider-area destinations mentioned by the developer; they are not moved into the local map.
